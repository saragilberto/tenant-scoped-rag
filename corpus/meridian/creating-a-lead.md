---
title: "Creating a Lead Manually"
category: "Pipeline"
version: "1.0"
visibility: empresa
---

A lead can be created manually from the leads screen by choosing "New lead" and filling in the
company name, contact name, and source. The source field is required and drives the attribution
reports later, so picking "Unknown" should be treated as a last resort rather than a default choice.
Once created, a lead sits in the "New" status until someone is assigned as its owner, either by
self-assigning or by a manager assigning it from the unowned leads queue. Leads that remain unowned
for more than seven days are surfaced in the weekly pipeline health report sent to team leads.
A lead converts into a contact and, optionally, a deal once it passes the qualification checklist
defined by the sales operations team for that workspace. Converting a lead does not delete it; the
original lead record is kept for attribution history and marked as converted, with a link to the
resulting contact record.
