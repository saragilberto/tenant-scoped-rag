## The four categories

Every open deal is assigned a forecast category: Pipeline, Best Case, Commit, or Omitted. This
category is separate from the pipeline stage — a deal can be in the Proposal stage but already marked
Commit if the sales rep is confident it will close.

## Who sets the category

The deal owner sets the forecast category manually; it does not change automatically based on stage.
Sales managers can override a rep's category during forecast review, and the override is recorded
with a note explaining the change, visible to the original owner.

## Omitted deals

Marking a deal Omitted removes it from forecast totals without closing or archiving it — this is
meant for deals that are technically still open but which the rep no longer expects to close in the
current forecasting period, such as a prospect who has gone unresponsive.

## Rolling up forecasts

Forecast totals roll up by team hierarchy, so a manager's forecast view always reflects the categories
their direct and indirect reports have set, recalculated live rather than on a nightly batch.
