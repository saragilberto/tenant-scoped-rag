## Setting it up

Two-factor authentication in Meridian uses a time-based one-time code generated by an authenticator
app. From the account security screen, choose "Enable two-factor authentication" and scan the
displayed QR code with your authenticator app of choice. Enter the six-digit code it produces to
confirm the pairing.

## Recovery codes

Meridian generates ten single-use recovery codes at setup time. Store them somewhere other than the
device running the authenticator app — losing both the device and the codes means account recovery
requires a workspace owner to manually reset your two-factor status from the member management
screen.

## Workspace-wide enforcement

A workspace owner can require two-factor authentication for every member. Once enforced, members
without it configured are shown a setup prompt on their next sign-in and cannot dismiss it
permanently, though they can postpone it up to three times before access is blocked.

## Lockout versus disabled

A two-factor lockout (three wrong codes in a row) is a temporary ten-minute hold, distinct from a
disabled account. If the countdown will not go away after ten minutes, the issue is most likely
device clock drift — authenticator codes are time-sensitive and a device more than thirty seconds
out of sync will keep producing rejected codes.
