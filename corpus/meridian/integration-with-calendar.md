---
title: "Syncing Meetings with Your Calendar"
category: "Integrations"
version: "1.0"
visibility: empresa
---

Connecting a calendar lets Meridian log meetings on the relevant contact or deal timeline
automatically, matching meeting attendees against known contacts by comparing calendar attendee
records rather than by name alone, which avoids mismatches when two contacts share a similar name.
Meetings with no matching attendee are left unlogged rather than attached to the wrong record. A
meeting can be manually linked afterward from the calendar sync screen if the automatic match missed
it, most commonly when a contact was added to the meeting after the calendar invite was first sent.
Canceling a meeting in the calendar removes it from the timeline within a few minutes, while
rescheduling updates the logged time without creating a duplicate entry.
