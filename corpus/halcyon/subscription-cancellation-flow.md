---
title: "How Subscription Cancellation Works"
category: "Billing"
version: "1.0"
visibility: empresa
---

Canceling a subscription can be scheduled for the end of the current billing period or applied
immediately, chosen at the time of cancellation, with immediate cancellation prorating a credit for
the unused remainder rather than issuing a cash refund. A canceled subscription's historical invoices
remain accessible indefinitely even though the subscription itself no longer bills. Reactivating a
subscription within thirty days of cancellation restores its original plan and pricing automatically;
reactivating after thirty days requires setting it up as a new subscription against whatever plan
versions are currently active, since older plan versions may no longer be offered. Cancellation
reason is captured through a required dropdown, feeding the churn reporting used by the billing
operations team.
