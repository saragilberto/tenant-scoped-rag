## When proration applies

Switching a customer between plans mid-cycle prorates both the unused portion of the old plan and the
remaining portion of the new plan, combined into a single adjustment on the next invoice rather than
issued as an immediate separate charge or refund.

## Upgrades versus downgrades

Upgrading mid-cycle charges the prorated difference immediately as a one-time line item, while
downgrading mid-cycle only applies a credit toward the next invoice, since Halcyon does not issue
partial refunds to the original payment method automatically.

## Disabling proration

Proration can be disabled per plan change, in which case the customer is billed the new plan's full
price starting on their next regular billing date, with no adjustment for the switch itself.

## Metered components and proration

Metered usage components are never prorated, since they are already billed based on actual usage
within the billing period rather than a fixed recurring amount.
