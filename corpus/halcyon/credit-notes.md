---
title: "Issuing Credit Notes"
category: "Billing"
version: "1.0"
visibility: restrito
---

A credit note reduces a customer's outstanding balance without reversing a specific charge the way a
refund does, and is typically used for goodwill adjustments or contract renegotiations rather than
for correcting a billing error. Credit notes apply automatically to the customer's next invoice in
the order they were issued, oldest first, and any unused balance carries forward indefinitely rather
than expiring. Unlike a refund, a credit note never touches the original payment method and cannot be
converted back into a cash refund once issued; reversing one requires issuing a new invoice adjustment
instead. Every credit note requires a reason selected from the same reason list used for refunds, for
consistent reporting across both adjustment types.
