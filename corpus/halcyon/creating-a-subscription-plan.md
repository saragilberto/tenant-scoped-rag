---
title: "Creating a Subscription Plan"
category: "Billing"
version: "1.3"
visibility: departamentos
---

A subscription plan is created from the plans screen by naming it, choosing a billing interval of
monthly, quarterly, or annual, and setting a base price per interval. Plans can optionally include
metered components billed on top of the base price, configured separately after the base plan is
saved. Once at least one customer is subscribed to a plan, its base price and interval become locked
to protect existing subscribers from unexpected changes; adjusting price for existing subscribers
requires creating a new plan version and migrating them explicitly rather than editing the original
plan in place. A plan can be archived once it has no active subscribers, which hides it from the
plan picker without deleting its historical billing records.
