---
title: "Updating a Customer's Payment Method"
category: "Billing"
version: "1.0"
visibility: empresa
---

A customer can update their own payment method through the self-service billing portal link sent in
any invoice email, without needing to contact support or sign in to a full account. A billing admin
can also update a payment method directly from the customer record, which takes effect on the very
next billing attempt including any dunning retry already in progress. Halcyon keeps the previous
payment method on file for thirty days after a new one is added, in case the new method needs to be
reverted, after which the old method is permanently removed. Updating a payment method does not
retroactively affect invoices already issued; it only changes what is charged going forward.
