title: "Customizing Invoice Templates"
category: "Billing"
version: "1.0"
visibility: empresa
---

An invoice template controls the logo, color accent, and footer text shown on customer-facing
invoices, configured once per workspace from the billing settings screen rather than per customer.
Changing the template only affects invoices generated after the change; previously issued invoices
keep the template that was active when they were created, since an invoice is meant to be a permanent
record of what was actually presented to the customer at the time. Line item descriptions on the
template can reference plan and metered component names directly, and updating a plan's display name
does update the description on future invoices, but again never on invoices already issued.
